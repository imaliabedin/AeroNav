Search an area

  1. Click four spots on the ground to mark the corners of the area to search. They can be in any order.
  2. The area turns green when it is valid (red: try other corners). The blue lines are the drone's planned lanes.
  3. Choose what was lost: wallet, keys, phone or purse.
  4. Press Start scan. The drone flies the lanes and photographs the ground straight below it, then goes back to spots that traffic was blocking.

Hold the right mouse button and use WASD to move the view. An AI model checks every photo for the lost object; images where it finds it are listed with their estimated position.
